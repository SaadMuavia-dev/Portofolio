About Farmevo

Farmevo is an international, AI-driven agriculture technology company, founded in Pakistan and now headquartered in New York with a growing footprint across North America. Its autonomous, drone-based remote-sensing platform — including LenseAI for crop monitoring and SylvaSense for forestry — turns satellite imagery, drone data, and sensor readings into plant stand counts, weed detection, fertilization maps, nutrient deficiency reports, crop health scores, and yield forecasts, with no piloting or GIS expertise required from the user.

As a QA Associate at Farmevo, I test and validate these AI-powered agri-tech tools, helping make sure the reports farmers and agribusinesses rely on stay accurate and dependable.
